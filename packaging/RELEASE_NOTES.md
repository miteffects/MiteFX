Free, open-source audio plugins: 19 of them, VST3 and CLAP, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Install:** `MiteFX-Windows-Setup.exe` · `MiteFX-macOS.pkg` (universal) · `MiteFX-Linux.tar.xz` (with `install.sh`) or `MiteFX-Linux.deb`.
`MiteFX-Windows.7z` and `MiteFX-macOS.tar.xz` are the same plugins to copy by hand. Installers are not signed yet: Windows asks you to click More info → Run anyway, macOS to click Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. No AU yet.
