MiteFX for Windows (64-bit): 18 plugins, VST3 and CLAP.

The easy way: MiteFX-Windows-Setup.exe (from the website) installs and uninstalls them for you.

By hand, from this folder
  Copy everything in VST3\ to   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Copy everything in CLAP\ to   C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

Needs a GPU with DirectX 12 or Vulkan (the editors are GPU drawn).
