MiteFX for Linux (x86-64): 18 plugins, VST3 and CLAP.

Install
  mkdir -p ~/.vst3 ~/.clap
  cp -r VST3/* ~/.vst3/
  cp -r CLAP/* ~/.clap/
Then rescan plugins in your DAW (REAPER, Bitwig, Ardour, ...).

Needs X11 (or XWayland) and a GPU with Vulkan.
