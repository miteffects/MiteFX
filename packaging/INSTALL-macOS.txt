The easy way: MiteFX-macOS.pkg (from the website) installs them, with nothing to clear by hand.
What follows is the manual way, from this folder.

MiteFX for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel): 18 plugins, VST3 and CLAP.

Install
  Copy everything in VST3/ to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Copy everything in CLAP/ to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/

The plugins are not notarized by Apple (they are free and unsigned), so macOS quarantines them.
Clear that once, in Terminal:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Mite*.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Mite*.clap
Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

Audio Unit (AU, for Logic Pro) is not included yet.
